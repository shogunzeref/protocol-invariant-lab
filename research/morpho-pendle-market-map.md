## INFERENCES

Labelled as inference because they combine several confirmed reads with an
interpretation that the reads alone do not force.

- **No live PT market prices collateral from an untimed AMM spot read.** Across 66 markets the classified paths contain 900s / 1800s TWAP windows, market-independent linear-discount feeds, or both. Inference rather than fact only because 9 paths terminate in an external feed whose internals were not verifiable (see UNKNOWN).
- **The `min()` aggregation in the Ojo PT feeds is the binding defence, not the TWAP.** A feed that reports `min(twap, linearDiscount)` cannot be raised by buying PT unless the TWAP leg is already the lower of the two -- and buying raises it toward the discount leg, at which point the discount leg caps it.
- **The linear-discount feeds are deliberately conservative, not merely manipulation-resistant.** They report `1 - discount * T`, a straight line strictly below the exponential the market prices, so borrowers on those markets systematically receive less capacity than the PT is worth.
- **The MetaOracle deviation timelocks are a governance circuit breaker, not a price filter.** They compare a primary and backup feed and gate switching between them behind a challenge/healing delay, which bounds how fast a compromised feed can take effect but does not smooth an individual feed.
